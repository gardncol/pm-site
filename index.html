How I work

Learned the hard way

01Backlogs are promises

A user story without real acceptance criteria is a wish. I write tickets engineers don't need to decode, then follow up until requirements actually land.

02Ship small, learn fast

Iterative releases beat perfect launches. I'd rather show rough work and correct course than burn a week polishing something that's off-track.

03Metrics before opinions

Track the KPIs that tell you whether something's working, and let them pick what's next. Roadmaps defended with numbers survive reorgs.

04Ask until it's obvious

I dig into domains I haven't worked in and keep asking good-natured questions until the why is as clear as the what. Engineers trust that.
